In December 2021 I started my internship at Vixteam, a software development company in Vitória, Brazil (a great company I have nothing but praise for), and with it came my first paycheck. Like any 21-year-old living with his mom (and still do), I already knew what my first purchase would be: a card game I had seen on TikTok a while before (thanks, @tiktokboardgames, the GOAT). It was Here To Slay.

I fell in love with it right away. I got all my friends to play, and many of them really liked it too (I think I lost everyone's first match... beginner's luck?).

One match in particular stuck with me. It was at an end-of-semester barbecue at a friend's house. The game started around 3 p.m., and I remember looking at my boyfriend, who was losing badly at that point. The match only ended two hours later, and guess who came out on top? Him. That's when I realized Here To Slay wasn't just any game: it had held everyone's attention, at the same table and in the same match, for hours.

Some time later the question came up: "why not make an online version of this game?". I mentioned it to the group, a lot of people liked the idea and... here we are. I started the project in September 2025 and have been working on it on and off ever since.

This is a passion project, for the game and for all the hours of fun it gave me. In the next posts, I'll talk about what it was like (and what it's still like) to build this version: the decisions, the mistakes and what I learned along the way.
