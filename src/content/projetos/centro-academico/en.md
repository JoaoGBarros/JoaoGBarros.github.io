I was elected Student Ombudsman Coordinator at the UFES IT Academic Center (CAI), representing more than 500 Computer Science and Engineering students.

During that time I negotiated directly with the department heads to secure a dedicated student lounge and funding for a building renovation. I also co-organized the 9th Regional School of Informatics with the Brazilian Computer Society (SBC), handling logistics and promotion for an event with 120+ attendees.
